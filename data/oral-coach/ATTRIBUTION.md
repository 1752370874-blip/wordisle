# Open training and teaching examples

CCPE-M movie conversations, Filip Radlinski, Krisztian Balog, Bill Byrne and Karthik Krishnamoorthi, Google LLC, 2019; https://github.com/google-research-datasets/ccpe ; Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The data was converted to USER/ASSISTANT text pairs and filtered for length; annotation and identifiers were removed.

Elispeak Common English Mistakes, https://github.com/elispeak/elispeak-common-english-mistakes ; MIT license (see ELISPEAK-LICENSE.txt). JSONL records are embedded in corpus.js without changes.

The examples are retrieved during a session to guide dialogue and optional feedback. They are not model fine-tuning.
